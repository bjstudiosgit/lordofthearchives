Clash Summary

Ren DMC faced Renaya in a highly anticipated battle that turned out to be a clean sweep. Renaya struggled with nerves and stumbled in all three rounds, failing to complete most of her verses. Ren DMC took advantage of this by delivering three highly polished, aggressive, and creative rounds. He used personal angles about Renaya's parenting, her internet presence, and compared her to Jack from Top Boy. Ren DMC won the battle easily, proving his status as a top contender.
